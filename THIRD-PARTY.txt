VOICE, SOUNDS AND MUSIC
The spoken clips in audio/<group>.mp3 are AI-generated with ElevenLabs Eleven v4
(the premade voice "Jessica"), made for this app through fal.ai when it was
built. They are not recordings or a voice clone of any real person.
The animal sounds, most game sound effects (audio/sfx.mp3) and the home-screen
song (audio/music-home.mp3) are AI-generated too: the sounds with ElevenLabs
Sound Effects v3 and the song with Google Lyria 3.5, also through fal.ai.
Taps, clicks and the Music Maker instruments are synthesized by the app at
runtime with the Web Audio API, which also stands in for any recording that
hasn't loaded. The app itself contacts no speech, sound or music service.
audio/manifest.json lists every spoken clip's text.
Until version 1.2.0 the voice was Kokoro-82M v1.0 (hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, Apache
License 2.0), rendered locally with kokoro-onnx 0.6.1 (MIT License).

PICTURES
Characters, scenes and the app icon are original SVG
drawings. Pictures of everyday things use the device's own emoji font.
Melodies in Music Maker (Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Mary Had a Little Lamb,
Row Row Row Your Boat, Old MacDonald) are traditional and in the public domain.

No analytics, ads, accounts, trackers or network services are used.
