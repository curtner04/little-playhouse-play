VOICE
The spoken clips in audio/*.mp3 are original AI-generated recordings rendered
locally at build time with Kokoro-82M v1.0, a blend of 65% af_bella and
35% af_heart (the same voice as Little Buttons). They are not recordings or a
voice clone of any real person. No speech model or service ships with the app.
Model: hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, Apache License 2.0 - https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
Build-time renderer: kokoro-onnx 0.6.1, MIT License - https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
audio/manifest.json lists every clip's text.

SOUNDS, MUSIC AND PICTURES
Sound effects and the background music are synthesized by the app at runtime
with the Web Audio API. Characters, scenes and the app icon are original SVG
drawings. Pictures of everyday things use the device's own emoji font.
Melodies in Music Maker (Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Mary Had a Little Lamb,
Row Row Row Your Boat, Old MacDonald) are traditional and in the public domain.

No analytics, ads, accounts, trackers or network services are used.
